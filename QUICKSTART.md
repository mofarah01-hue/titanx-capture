# Titan X Capture: quick start

This page mirrors the setup screens in the app. You need a Mac, Google Chrome, and the Titan X sensors. Setup takes about 15 minutes, once.

## One time setup

**1. Use Chrome.** The page only works in Google Chrome. If you do not have it, download it from google.com/chrome. Safari cannot talk to Bluetooth sensors.

**2. Bookmark the page.** Open the link Mohammad sent you in Chrome. Press **Cmd** and **D** together, then click Done. From now on, open the bookmark.

**3. Turn on Bluetooth.** Click the Control Center icon at the top right of the screen (two small switches). Click Bluetooth so it turns blue. The first time you connect a sensor, the Mac asks whether Chrome may use Bluetooth. Click **Allow**.

**4. Install Google Drive for desktop.** Download it from google.com/drive/download, open the installer and follow it. Sign in with your Google account. A Google Drive icon appears at the top of the screen.

**5. Add the shared Sa7a folder to your Drive.** Open drive.google.com in Chrome and click **Shared with me**. Right click the **Sa7a** folder, choose **Organize**, then **Add shortcut to My Drive**, then pick My Drive. Without this shortcut the Mac cannot see the folder, and your recordings cannot reach Mohammad.

**6. Pick your save folder.** In the app, click **Choose folder**. In the window that opens, click **Google Drive** on the left, open **My Drive**, click **Sa7a** once, then click **Select**. The app checks that it can save there and that it is the shared folder. If Chrome asks, choose **Allow on every visit**.

**7. Test with a sensor.** Put the headgear on someone. Press the button on one sensor so its light blinks. Click **Connect**, and in the popup pick the sensor whose last four characters match its sticker. The step ticks when heart rate arrives, within about 30 seconds.

**8. You are ready.**

## Every session

1. Plug the laptop in. Keep the lid open and the Titan X window visible.
2. Open the bookmark and click **Start a session**. Tick the checklist.
3. On each athlete's panel, click **Connect** and pick that athlete's sensor in the popup. One popup per sensor. Recording starts by itself.
4. When something happens, press an event button: **Still, Light, Heavy, Adjusted, HR looks wrong**, or **Mark**. You can type a note first. Pick an athlete under **Mark for** if the event is about one person.
5. When done, click **End session**. Leave the Mac on and online for a few minutes so Google Drive can send the recording to Mohammad.

## If something goes wrong

Click **Help** at the top right of the app. Each problem has one short fix. If it still does not work, click **Send this to Mohammad** at the bottom of Help, then text or email him the file it names.

To update the app, reload the page (Cmd and R). A banner tells you when a new version is out.
